# Project roles

The Apptonomia metaproject has **three differentiated roles**. Each
has its own space in the repo:

| Role | Who they are | How they participate | Where they look first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👤 **End user** (visitor of `apptonomia.uk`) | The person who lands on the portal and clicks through to one of the six apps of the suite | Visits `apptonomia.uk`, reads the six cards, opens the chosen app on its own domain. **Does not participate** in development. | The landing — nothing else to read |
| ❤️ **Support / family** | Helps an end user navigate the suite | Picks the right app of the suite for a need; helps install it on the device | Each suite app's `README.md` |
| 💻 **Build / developer** | Maintains the landing and the cross-project plumbing | Edits `index.html`, `js/`, `_headers`, `wrangler.toml`; runs `node scripts/sync-graphify-skill.js`; reviews cross-project PRs | [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) |

> 💡 **The end user is the audience, and their experience is
> central**. The Apptonomia landing exists for one reason: to help
> the visitor pick the right app of the suite and open it on its
> own domain. Landing design, copy, accessibility and SEO all
> follow that single purpose.

## 🤝 Cross-project coordination

Apptonomia is the **metaproject root** of the Miralante suite. Its
contributors coordinate the seven sibling repos (Calculia,
Memofun, Okeymoney, Sinonimia, Teclatlon, Routime, and any future
addition) through:

- **The canonical-source table** in
  [`../../CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) §A.1 — one row per topic,
  one canonical doc per topic.
- **The graphify meta-graph** at
  [`../../graphify-out-meta/`](../../graphify-out-meta/) — one
  node per sibling project plus similarity / hierarchy edges,
  regenerated by `node scripts/sync-graphify-skill.js update
  --apply`.
- **The metaproject sync script** —
  `scripts/sync-graphify-skill.js` — the only sanctioned
  cross-project mutation tool. It is intentionally minimal
  (Node, no deps, sha256-compares `SKILL.md`) so a future
  maintainer can read it in five minutes.

> 👉 **Want to participate?** Start with
> [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) (at the repo root).
> It explains the GitHub workflow, labels, branches and per-profile
> checklists.
