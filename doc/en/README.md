# Apptonomia

**Web application for occupational therapy activities for people with intellectual disability.**

---

## What is Apptonomia?

Apptonomia is a digital tool designed for people with intellectual disability to practice daily living skills **independently**, without needing a professional by their side at all times.

The app offers **57 activities** organized into **6 thematic areas**:

| Module | What it works on | Examples |
|--------|-----------------|----------|
| 🎯 **Coordination and motor skills** | Precise hand and finger movements | Catch objects, Piano, Tracing |
| 📋 **Autonomy and home** | Independent living at home and in the community | Routines, Cooking, Emergencies |
| 🧠 **Memory and attention** | Remembering and focusing | Pairs, Differences, Patterns |
| 🔢 **Thinking and counting** | Reasoning and mathematics | Riddles, Clock, Dominoes |
| 💬 **Language and words** | Communication and vocabulary | Jokes, Sayings, Categories |
| 💜 **Emotions** | Recognizing and managing emotions | How do I feel?, Calm |

---

## Key features

### ✅ Designed for independence

- **Easy Reading**: short sentences, everyday vocabulary, one idea per screen
- **No pressure**: no timers, no negative scores, no punishment
- **Positive reinforcement**: celebrates correct answers with friendly sounds and animations

### ✅ Accessible to everyone

- **Large buttons**: minimum 64×64 pixels
- **Large text**: clear readable font (Atkinson Hyperlegible)
- **High contrast**: light colors on white background
- **Audio available**: every important text can be heard with a 🔊 button

### ✅ Works offline

- **Offline**: can be used without connection once installed
- **No personal data**: no registration, email or password required
- **Free**: no payment needed

### ✅ In two languages

- 🇪🇸 **Español** (default)
- 🇬🇧 **English** (can be changed from the menu)

## Getting started

### 1. Open the app

Visit **[apptonomia.web.app](https://apptonomia.web.app)** or open `site/index.html` from a local server.

### 2. Choose an activity

On the main screen you'll see 6 modules. Tap the module you're interested in and then choose an activity.

### 3. Change language

Tap the language button (🇪🇸 or 🇬🇧) at the top of the screen.

### 4. Install on device (optional)

If you want the app on your phone or tablet like a regular app:

1. Open the app in the browser
2. Look for "Install" or "Add to Home Screen" option
3. Confirm the installation

---

## Example of use

Imagine you want to practice telling time. In the **🔢 Thinking and counting** module, choose **The Clock**. The app will show a clock and ask "What time is it?". You can:

1. **Look at** the clock
2. **Listen to** the question with the 🔊 button
3. **Tap** the correct answer

If you get it right, a brief celebration appears. If not, you'll receive an encouraging message: "Almost. Try again!".

## Featured activities

### For working at home

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| **My Routines** | Review daily routines: wake up, get dressed, eat... |
| **The House** | Put in order the steps for household tasks |
| **Emergencies** | Learn when to call 112 and what to say |
| **Shopping** | Practice at the supermarket |

### For thinking and solving

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Pairs** | Find the matching cards |
| **Riddles** | Discover what it is from clues |
| **Patterns** | Complete series of shapes or colors |
| **Dominoes** | Play a game of dominoes |

### For emotions

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| **How do I feel?** | Learn to recognize emotions |
| **Calm** | Guided breathing and relaxation exercises |
| **Among Friends** | Solve situations with friends |
